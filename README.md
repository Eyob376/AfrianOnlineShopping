## 📌 About African Online Shopping

**African Online Shopping (AOS)** is a fast-growing business management and online marketplace platform designed to empower businesses with modern digital tools to manage their daily operations, advertise their products, connect with customers, and expand their business opportunities.

AOS provides each registered business with a dedicated webpage and an advanced business management system where business owners can record, organize, manage, and monitor their daily business activities.

In addition to business management, AOS provides an online marketplace where registered businesses can advertise and showcase their products. Customers can browse available products, place orders directly through individual business webpages, and submit their contact and delivery information.

Business owners receive customer orders and the relevant customer information through their business management environment, allowing them to review, process, and manage orders directly.

Our mission is to simplify business management, connect businesses with customers, and create opportunities for businesses to grow locally and internationally.

## 🚀 Core Features

AOS combines two major services into one integrated platform.

### 1. Business Management System

Every registered business receives access to its own business management environment, with tools to organize and monitor daily operations.

Key features include:

- **Business Management:** Dedicated business accounts and management environments.
- **Daily Sales Management:** Record, organize, and monitor daily sales transactions.
- **Sales History:** Review historical transactions and track business performance.
- **Inventory Management:** Organize products and monitor available inventory.
- **Stock Management:** Record incoming and outgoing stock and monitor product quantities.
- **Creditors Management:** Maintain records of money owed to suppliers and other creditors.
- **Debtors Management:** Track customer debts, outstanding balances, and credit transactions.
- **Financial Management:** Maintain business financial and transaction records.
- **Business Reports:** Generate reports to monitor sales, inventory, and financial activities.
- **Transaction Management:** Maintain organized records of business transactions.

### 2. Online Marketplace & Product Advertising

AOS provides registered businesses with an opportunity to advertise their products and reach customers through the official website.

Key features include:

- **Product Advertising:** Businesses can advertise and showcase their products online.
- **Dedicated Business Webpages:** Every registered business has its own webpage.
- **Product Listings:** Display product photographs, descriptions, prices, and availability.
- **Online Customer Orders:** Customers can submit orders directly through individual business webpages.
- **Customer Information:** Businesses receive customer-submitted names, telephone numbers, delivery addresses, and relevant order details.
- **Order Management:** Business owners can review and manage incoming customer orders.
- **Direct Business-to-Customer Connection:** Connect customers directly with the businesses offering the products.

### 3. Payment Arrangements

AOS supports two payment arrangements:

- **Direct Payments:** Customers can arrange payments directly with individual businesses.
- **AOS Payments:** Customers can use payment arrangements provided or facilitated through AOS, where available.

These options provide flexibility for businesses and customers.
