# Contributing to African Online Shopping (AOS)

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **African Online Shopping (AOS)**!

AOS is a business management and online marketplace platform designed to help businesses organize their daily operations, manage sales and inventory, maintain financial records, and showcase their products to customers.

Our goal is to expand opportunities for businesses across Africa and worldwide through accessible digital technology.

We welcome contributions from developers, designers, business professionals, testers, and anyone who wants to help improve the platform.

## 1. How You Can Contribute

There are several ways to contribute to AOS:

- **Software Development:** Help improve existing features and develop new functionality.
- **Bug Reports:** Identify and report technical problems.
- **Feature Suggestions:** Recommend useful improvements for businesses and customers.
- **UI/UX Design:** Help make the platform easier to use.
- **Testing:** Test features and report unexpected behavior.
- **Documentation:** Improve user guides, technical documentation, and instructions.
- **Business Feedback:** Share ideas that can help businesses manage their operations more effectively.

## 2. Before You Start

Before contributing:

1. Review the project README.md.
2. Understand the purpose and functionality of AOS.
3. Check existing issues and discussions to avoid duplicate work.
4. For major changes, discuss your proposal with the project maintainers before starting.

## 3. Reporting Bugs

If you discover a bug:

1. Clearly describe the problem.
2. Explain the steps needed to reproduce it.
3. Describe what you expected to happen.
4. Explain what actually happened.
5. Include screenshots or error messages when appropriate.
6. Provide relevant browser, device, or environment information.

Please do not include passwords, customer information, government-issued identification, payment details, or other confidential information in bug reports.

## 4. Suggesting New Features

We welcome ideas that improve AOS.

When suggesting a feature, explain:

- What the proposed feature should do.
- What problem it will solve.
- Who will benefit from it.
- How it could improve business management or the customer shopping experience.

Examples include improved inventory tracking, sales reporting, order management, financial records, and additional payment options.

## 5. Submitting Code Contributions

If you want to contribute code:

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a new branch for your changes.
3. Make your changes following the project's existing coding conventions.
4. Test your changes.
5. Commit your work with a clear and meaningful commit message.
6. Push your branch to your fork.
7. Submit a Pull Request to the AOS repository.

Please keep contributions focused and avoid including unrelated changes.

## 6. Coding Standards

Contributors should:

- Write clean, readable, and maintainable code.
- Use meaningful names for variables, functions, and files.
- Follow the existing project structure and coding style.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Document complex functionality when appropriate.
- Avoid introducing breaking changes without discussing them with maintainers.

## 7. Security and Privacy Requirements

AOS handles important business and customer information. Security and privacy must be considered in every contribution.

Contributors must:

- Never expose passwords, API keys, access tokens, or database credentials.
- Never commit `.env` files or other files containing secrets.
- Protect customer names, phone numbers, and delivery addresses.
- Protect business financial records, inventory information, and government-issued business identification.
- Respect business-level data separation and access permissions.
- Follow appropriate Supabase database security practices, including Row Level Security (RLS), where applicable.
- Never access or disclose another business's private information without authorization.

For security vulnerabilities, please contact the AOS team privately rather than publishing sensitive details in a public issue.

## 8. Testing

Before submitting a contribution:

- Check that your changes work as expected.
- Test relevant existing functionality.
- Verify that your changes do not negatively affect other features.
- Check for errors and unexpected behavior.
- Include details of the testing you performed in your Pull Request.

## 9. Pull Request Guidelines

Every Pull Request should include:

- A clear title.
- A description of the changes.
- The reason for making the changes.
- Screenshots when applicable.
- Testing information.
- Any known limitations or potential issues.

AOS maintainers may review, request changes, approve, or decline contributions.

## 10. Code of Conduct

All contributors are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome different ideas and perspectives.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and abusive behavior.
- Help maintain a positive and collaborative environment.

## 11. Intellectual Property and Licensing

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

- You have the right to submit the work.
- Your contribution does not knowingly violate another party's intellectual property rights.
- You agree that your contribution may be reviewed and incorporated into AOS, subject to the project's applicable contribution terms and licensing arrangements.

Please contact the project maintainers if you need clarification about ownership or licensing before contributing.
